Phase 4

In short

  1. HR starts a review cycle (e.g. "H1 2026") for chosen employees.
  2. The employee rates themselves and comments; the manager adds comments and the final rating (1–5).
  3. HR assigns courses with a due date; employees start and complete them, with a score.

  • An employee gets only one review per cycle (duplicates are skipped).
  • A Team Lead sees and reviews direct reports only. Reviews due within 7 days or overdue raise a notification.

Learning

Course categories: Compliance, Sales, Management, Finance, Culture, Technical, Safety, Soft Skills (HR can add more); the same course is never assigned twice to the same person.

Not in the frontend yet

  • There is no real "cycle" or "course catalogue" — both are just text on each record. The backend adds proper tables.
  • Goals are free text; no KPIs, 360° feedback or calibration yet. The 11 performance reports in the Reports module expect more data than exists.
