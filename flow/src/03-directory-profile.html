Phase 1

In short

  1. HR, Office Admin and Super Admin see and edit everyone. A Team Lead sees direct reports. An employee sees only themselves.
  2. Bank and salary details are visible only to HR and Super Admin.
  3. Every change is recorded field by field; employees ask for changes through Requests.

Editing a profile

  • Never editable directly: work email, employee code and employment status (status changes only through onboarding and offboarding).
  • Employees change other personal details through Requests (chapter 4).
  • Org chart: HR can drag a person onto a new manager. The system blocks loops (someone reporting to their own report).
  • Identity numbers and bank details are encrypted in the backend and shown masked (e.g. ••••••234F) unless the viewer may reveal them.

Not in the frontend yet

  • Edits are saved in the browser only; uploaded files are not stored anywhere.
  • The employee's own mobile change is not saved.
