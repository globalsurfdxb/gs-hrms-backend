In short

  1. Locations (Dubai, Kochi) carry the country rules.
  2. Companies work in one or both locations; departments belong to a company; designations are job titles in a department.
  3. Every employee is placed in a location, company, department and designation.

How the pieces fit

Adding and changing

  1. Locations decide the country rules: which ID fields an employee has, currency, bank format, holidays and leave types.
  2. Companies are business units. A company works in Dubai, Kochi or both.
  3. Departments belong to a company and list the locations they operate in. When an employee is placed, the department must belong to their company and operate in their location.
  4. Designations are the job titles inside a department.
  5. Every change is recorded in the audit log (module "Organization").

Not in the frontend yet

  • Changes are kept only in the browser and are lost on refresh.
  • Removing a company also removes its departments and designations, even if employees still use them. Backend rule: block or deactivate instead of deleting when records are in use.
  • Employees store company/department/designation as names; the backend uses ids, so renaming is safe.
