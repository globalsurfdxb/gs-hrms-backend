Employee Management

5 · Onboarding

How a new joiner becomes an employee in the system — either typed in by HR or carried over from an accepted job offer, with the joiner filling in their own details through a private joining form.

Used by

Backend
Phase 1

In short

  1. HR starts an onboarding by hand or from an accepted job offer, which fills most of the form.
  2. HR can send the joiner a private joining form link; the joiner fills in their personal, identity, bank and family details, and the wizard is filled from it.
  3. The wizard saves automatically; unfinished onboardings appear under In progress and can be resumed or discarded.
  4. On submit the employee is created with status Onboarding. HR then approves it or sends it back with a note.
  5. After approval, payroll is told to set up the salary.

The flow

The joining form

  • One form per joiner. Sending again keeps the same link and what they already typed.
  • The joiner must give names, date of birth, nationality, contacts, address, identity numbers, bank details (account number typed twice), PF for Kochi, one emergency contact, a passport-size photo, and tick the declaration.
  • Documents HR already collected before the offer letter are not asked for again.
  • The email uses the Joining form template under Hiring → Operations → Email templates.

Approval

HR, Office Admin or Super Admin opens Review & approve, sees who is joining (code, role, position, location, joining date, work email) and approves — or sends it back, which needs a note on what to fix. Both decisions are recorded in the audit log.

UAE visa tracker (Dubai hires)

While the visa is being processed, HR ticks off 20 stages in 5 groups: Preparation & documents → Work permit (MOHRE) → Entry permit (GDRFA) → Medical & insurance → Emirates ID & residence.

When the hire comes from Hiring

  • Salary lines are matched: Housing → Accommodation, Transport → Transportation. Kochi offers are yearly, so amounts are divided by 12.
  • Employment type: Full-time → Permanent, Contract → Contract, Intern → Probation.
  • The offer must be Accepted and not already used for an employee.
  • Documents collected and verified before the offer letter, and the background-verification referral, are shown so nobody asks for them again.
  • If the candidate backs out after accepting, HR closes the offer as declined with a reason; the position reopens.

Not in the frontend yet — decisions needed

  • Approval is a single HR step. The Manager review in the configured chain is shown but not enforced, and approval does not move the joiner from Onboarding to Active. The lead must confirm the chain.
  • Nothing stops the HR user who submitted an onboarding from approving it.
  • The joining-form link never expires and the due date is not enforced — the backend must expire it.
  • Statutory salary figures are indicative; payroll must confirm the rates before the first pay run.
  • The visa tracker, asset choices and reminder settings live only in the draft and are never saved to the employee.
  • Uploaded files (wizard and joining form) are recorded by name only.
