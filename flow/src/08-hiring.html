8 · Hiring

From "we need a person" to "the person joined": requisition approval, job posting, candidate pipeline, interviews, pre-offer documents, offer letter, and the hand-over to onboarding.

In short

  1. A hiring manager asks for a position (requisition) — a Team Lead only for their own team. They pick a designation or ask for a new one. It is approved by the hiring manager, HR and management.
  2. HR turns the approved requisition into a job. The Talent Acquisition Manager chooses the recruiter who runs it. HR posts the job on the website and job boards by hand.
  3. Candidates move through Applied → Screening → Technical → HR interview → Offer. Interviews are scheduled with a panel.
  4. Before the offer, HR collects and verifies the candidate's documents and a background-verification referral (or skips this with a reason).
  5. HR sends an offer letter as a private link. The candidate accepts by signing and typing their name, or declines with a reason.
  6. An accepted offer becomes a Hired candidate, and HR starts onboarding with the form already filled.

A · Requisition — asking for a position

  • Nobody approves their own requisition: whoever raised it cannot decide its steps. Two exceptions: the hiring manager decides their own step (automatically when they submit it themselves), and a Super Admin confirms the Management step of their own requisition once the hiring manager and HR have approved.
  • The HR step is decided by HR or Office Admin; the Management step by a Super Admin, who may also decide any other step.
  • Designation first: the designation is chosen right after the department and fills the job title. A requested new designation stays a request until the requisition is fully approved; then it joins the department. A rejected requisition adds nothing.
  • A Team Lead whose department works in both countries chooses where the role is based.

B · Job and candidate pipeline

  • Interviews: round (Screening, Technical, HR, Final), date, time, duration, mode (In-person, Teams, Phone) and panel. The system warns when a panel member already has an interview at that time. Scheduling moves the candidate forward to that stage. Interviews can be rescheduled, completed or cancelled.
  • Emails per stage are either sent automatically, offered to HR ("ask"), or off. Template fields such as candidate name, job title and interview time are filled in automatically.
  • Job status: Draft → Open ⇄ On hold → Closed; a closed job can be reopened while positions are left to fill. Filled is set automatically when the last position is filled. Every change, and every recruiter change, is kept in the job's History tab. A job cannot be edited after it is created (frontend).
  • No candidate can be moved while the job is Closed or Filled.
  • A candidate is added once (same email or phone = already on file) and applies only to an Open job, once per job.
  • Own jobs: a Recruiter and a Team Lead work only with jobs where they are the recruiter or the hiring manager — that pipeline, those candidates, and their salary. A candidate they add goes straight onto one of those jobs, and the "already on file" check never shows them someone else's candidate. Opening, holding or closing a job and choosing its recruiter stay with HR and the Talent Acquisition team.
  • While an offer letter is out with the candidate, they cannot be rejected, withdrawn, put on hold or moved back from Offer — HR withdraws the offer first.

C · Pre-offer documents

  • UAE checklist: passport, education certificates (attested if available), experience letters, last 3 payslips; residence visa / Emirates ID and resignation acceptance if available.
  • India checklist: education certificates (10th, 12th, degree), Aadhaar, PAN, experience letters, last 3 payslips; resignation acceptance if available.
  • The offer button stays disabled until the documents are verified or the check is waived. Verified papers and the referral are carried into the joining form, so they are not asked for twice.

D · Offer and hand-over

  • Offer letters: in the UAE every company (GS IT, GS Digital, GS AV, GS Security) has its own letter template; India has one. The letter's company name and footer follow the company that issues the offer (taken from the job). HR uploads a .docx/.html file or edits the text; letters already prepared keep their text.
  • Recruiter: every job has a recruiter who runs it, chosen by the Talent Acquisition Manager (or a Super Admin; HR only on older jobs without a TA Manager). The new recruiter is notified and the change is kept in the job's history.

Who can do what

WhoCan
HR, Super AdminEverything: requisitions, jobs, all candidates, documents, offers, email templates, offer-letter templates, reports
Talent Acquisition ManagerAll of hiring (no delete); chooses the recruiter of every new job
RecruiterJobs they run: pipeline, candidates, interviews, documents, offers, and salary on those jobs
Team Lead, Office AdminRaise a requisition (only for their own department or one they head, as hiring manager — Office Admin too, since it has no Jobs access) and follow it; a Team Lead approves the Hiring Manager step of requisitions they manage and sees salary on their jobs
Any employeeInterviews they are on (/my/interviews); referral links for referral-eligible jobs and their referrals' progress
CandidateTheir own documents link and offer link only — no account

Kochi offers show the India breakup automatically: EPF 12% of Basic (capped at INR 1,800), Kerala professional tax, employer PF and gratuity.
