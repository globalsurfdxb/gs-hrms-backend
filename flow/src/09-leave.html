In short

  1. The employee picks a leave type and dates. The system counts working days only (weekends and public holidays are skipped).
  2. The system checks balance, overlaps, notice, documents and eligibility before the request is accepted.
  3. The request goes to the reporting manager. If there is none, it goes up the chain: manager's manager → HR → Super Admin.
  4. Approved leave is deducted from the balance. Pending leave is shown as "pending" and reserved.

Applying and approving

What the system checks

  • Not more than 30 days in the past; no overlap with other pending or approved leave.
  • Notice: Annual leave 30 days ahead, Earned leave 7 days ahead (warning only).
  • Sick leave certificate: Dubai — 2 days or more, or a Monday; Kochi — more than 2 days.
  • Eligibility: maternity (female), paternity/parental, Hajj once per service after 1 year, Umrah once a year, marriage leave once, study leave after 2 years. Restricted festive leave is exactly 1 day.
  • Annual leave plan: at most 3 blocks a year, each at least 7 days, at least 90 days apart.
  • The approver is never the requester, and never someone who reports to the requester.
