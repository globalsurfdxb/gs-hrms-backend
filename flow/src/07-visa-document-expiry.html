7 · Visa and document expiry

Keeps every dated document (visa, Emirates ID, passport, labour card, licences…) valid, by reminding the right people before it expires and recording each renewal.

Used by
HR Office Admin register and renewals · Employee own documents · Team Lead team view
Frontend
/visa, /team/visa, /employee-expiry, /my/expiry, /team/expiry, /admin/reminders, lib/reminderRules.ts, lib/expiryRegister.ts
Backend
Phase 1 (daily reminder job)

In short

  1. Every dated document has an expiry date and a reminder schedule (for example 90, 60, 30 and 7 days before).
  2. Each day the system sends the reminders that are due — once per step — to the employee and HR, and to the manager near the end.
  3. When the document is renewed, HR enters the new expiry date and the reminders start again.

Rules

  • Status: OK · Expiring soon (90 days or less) · Expired.
  • Mandatory for compliance: residence visa, Emirates ID, labour card, passport.
  • The visa register covers Dubai only and excludes inactive employees; Kochi shows "not applicable".
  • A renewal must have a new expiry date in the future and later than the current one.
  • A dated document only counts as valid when its file is uploaded.
  • HR can also send a reminder manually; it always includes the manager.

Not in the frontend yet

  • Reminders are only listed on screen — no email is sent and nothing runs automatically.
  • Admins can switch schedules on/off but cannot change the days or recipients yet.
