Phase 1

In short

  1. HR opens a separation case with the reason and notice period; the last working day is calculated.
  2. Five clearance items are completed: handover, assets returned, access revoked, finance, exit interview.
  3. HR approves and completes the case — the person becomes Inactive and can no longer sign in.

The flow

Statuses

Rules

  • Reasons: Resignation, Termination, End of contract, Retirement, Other.
  • Notice: 0, 15, 30, 60 or 90 days. Last working day = resignation date + notice (HR can override).
  • Only Active employees without an open case can be offboarded.
  • A Team Lead can see their direct reports' cases but cannot change them.
  • The separation register can be exported to CSV.
