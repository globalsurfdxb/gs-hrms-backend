In short

  1. Employees check in and out on the web or on a biometric device.
  2. Each day the system works out a status: present, late, half day, on leave, holiday, weekly off, missing punch, absent…
  3. Mistakes are fixed with a regularisation request approved by the manager.
  4. At month end HR resolves exceptions, locks the month and exports the figures for payroll.

A normal day

  • Planned late login, early departure or shift change can be requested in advance (today or later) and then do not count against the employee.
  • Regularisation: not for future dates or weekly offs; one per date. HR can also correct times directly (reason + attachment required).
  • Ramadan (Dubai): reduced working hours for the configured period.

Overtime and month end

Occurrences and discipline

Repeated late logins or early departures count as occurrences: 2 → verbal warning, 3 → first written, 6 → second written, 8 → final, 10 → termination review. Unauthorised absence escalates faster (1, 2, 3, 4). The system only recommends; HR confirms every outcome.

Not in the frontend yet

  • Past attendance is generated as sample data; nothing is really recorded. No biometric device is connected.
  • Approval chains for unpaid leave and overtime are described but each approval is a single step today.
