0 · System overview

GS HRMS is one system for every Global Surf employee in Dubai (UAE) and Kochi (India), across four companies: GS IT, GS Digital, GS AV and GS Security. It covers the whole working life of an employee — from the moment a position is requested until the person leaves.

The employee journey

Who uses the system

RoleSeesTypical work
EmployeeMy Space onlyOwn profile, leave, attendance, requests, payslips, claims, documents
Team LeadMy Space + TeamApproves direct reports' leave and requests; raises job requisitions; interviews
HRMy Space + Organization + Operations + Reports + AdministrationOnboarding, offboarding, visas, hiring, leave operations, approvals
Office AdminSame as HRCompany structure, files, document expiry, requests (cannot delete)
Super AdminEverythingRoles & permissions, final approvals, system settings

Any employee can also be an interviewer (sees only candidates they interview) and a referrer (shares job links and follows their referrals). Candidate people outside the company use only the public careers page and the offer link.

How to read the diagrams

  • Rounded boxes are steps; diamonds are decisions (yes / no).
  • Box colour shows who does the step:
    EmployeeTeam Lead / managerHRAdmin / Super AdminCandidateSystem (automatic)Stopped / rejected
  • State diagrams show the statuses a record moves through, e.g. Pending → Approved.
  • Red boxes titled "Not in the frontend yet" list behaviour the prototype only shows or does not do. The backend must implement it, or the lead must decide it.
