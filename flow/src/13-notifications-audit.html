In short

  1. When something happens (a request is raised, an offer is accepted…), the right people get a notification.
  2. Some notifications are time-based (a document expiring, an interview in one hour) and are checked automatically.
  3. All approvals follow workflow chains that admins can see (and, in the backend, change).
  4. Every change is written to the audit log, which can never be edited or deleted.

Audit log

Recorded modules include Security (sign-ins, exports), Employee, Bank, Salary, Organization, Access (roles and permissions), Approvals, Requests, Expense, Hiring, Leave & Attendance, Documents, Performance and Learning.

Not in the frontend yet

  • Notification rules and workflow switches are only displayed — nothing reads them. No email is ever sent.
  • Approval chains cannot be changed. The backend adds one shared workflow engine that admins can configure.
  • The frontend audit log lives in the browser; the backend version is permanent and protected by the database.
