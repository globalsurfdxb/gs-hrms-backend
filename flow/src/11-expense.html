Phase 4

In short

  1. The employee enters category, amount, date, project, description and attaches a receipt.
  2. The claim goes to their reporting manager (the General Manager's claims go to Super Admin).
  3. The approver approves or rejects with a note. The employee can withdraw while it is pending.

The flow

Rules

  • Categories (managed by HR/Admin) have a limit per currency (AED for Dubai, INR for Kochi) and can be switched off.
  • Receipt required when the amount is above the category limit or above 200 AED / 2,000 INR, whichever is lower.
  • Totals on the dashboard are kept per currency — AED and INR are never added together.
  • HR and Admin can file a claim on behalf of someone else.
