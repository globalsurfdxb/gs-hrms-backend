Phase 1

In short

  1. An employee raises a request to change personal details or to get a letter (e.g. salary certificate).
  2. Information updates go to the manager; everything else goes to HR.
  3. On approval, the new values are written to the profile automatically and recorded in the audit log.

The flow

Request types

TypeGoes toExample
Information UpdateReporting managerPersonal mobile or email, marital status, blood group, gender, nationality, name, date of birth
Document RequestHRSalary certificate, employment letter, experience letter, NOC, visa support letter, bank letter
Address ChangeHRCurrent or permanent address
Bank Detail UpdateHRNew account / IBAN
Other RequestHRAnything else

Who can decide

  • Nobody can decide their own request.
  • HR and Super Admin: any request.
  • Office Admin: any request except Bank Detail Update.
  • Team Lead: only manager-routed requests from their direct reports.

Statuses: Pending → Approved or Pending → Rejected. New values are applied only when approved.

Not in the frontend yet

  • Requested letters are never produced — the backend will generate them as PDFs from templates.
  • Bank detail changes should also notify the employee by email (protection against fraud).
